What will you play?

Connect your MIDI device, then choose it in MIDI in at the top. No device? Play with the computer keys, or click the keys and pads.

Have a song as a MIDI file? Load it with Load .mid below.

Two devices (a keyboard and a drum kit)? Once switched on, open Routing at the top to send each one to its instrument.

Advanced: who plays what

You play You · keys and You · drums (as Keyboard / Drums / Both at the top says; the one you are not playing, the device plays). Instruments 1–4 are played by the device along with you (MIDI channels 3–6); the rest are left out. ▶ plays an instrument alone from the section's start.
